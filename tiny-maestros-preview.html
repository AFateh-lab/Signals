Questions

Things parents ask

Do I need to know music myself?

No. Every lesson comes with a one-page parent guide written in plain language. If you can press play and clap along, you have everything you need. Plenty of our families are learning alongside their children.

Does my child need an instrument?

Not for Season One. Everything is voice, clapping and listening — which is exactly how music teachers begin. From Season Two a simple keyboard or xylophone helps, but it’s never required.

What ages is this really for?

Five to ten. Season One suits five to six, Season Two seven to eight, Season Three nine to ten. Children who start older simply move through the early seasons faster.

Is this the same as the free videos?

The lessons on YouTube are free and always will be. Membership adds the printables, backing tracks, teacher guides and an ad-free player that keeps everything in the right order — which matters more than it sounds when you’re teaching a sequence.

Can I use this in my classroom?

Yes. The Classroom plan covers up to 35 students and includes a printable licence. The curriculum follows the Kodály sequence, so it should sit neatly alongside what you already teach.

How do I cancel?

One email, or one click in your receipt. No phone calls, no retention scripts. See our refund policy for the details.
