Pattern Scanner — every timeframe, one coin

Wedges, double tops and bottoms, triangles, head & shoulders, flags · mechanically detected
Detection is mechanical; confirmation is not a forecast. Patterns are matched against one fixed geometric definition — swing pivots, least-squares trendlines, a tolerance you control. Confirmed means the breakout close has already happened, nothing more. Unlike the Open/Close Cross on the scanner page, these patterns have not been walk-forward tested in this toolkit, so no edge is claimed for them in either direction.
Pick a coin and press Scan.
Data: Binance public REST, USD-M futures (fapi.binance.com) or spot (api.binance.com). No key, no account. 13 timeframes; the six marked * are aggregated from 5m/1h/1d candles on exact epoch boundaries. The in-progress candle is always discarded, so nothing on this page repaints. Trendlines are least-squares fits through detected swing pivots — a different pivot width gives different lines, which is exactly why the width is an input and not a hidden constant.

Live liquidations are real executed force-orders from the !forceOrder@arr stream. Liquidation levels are computed, not observed — Binance publishes no map of where stops sit, and the per-tier maintenance margin table requires an API key, so a flat rate is assumed. Treat levels as arithmetic on an assumed average entry, not as known positions.

Volume Profile — where the coin actually traded

Volume by price, TPO / time at price, point of control, value area, naked POCs
Built from candles, not from every tick. Binance publishes no historical volume-at-price feed, so each candle's volume is spread across the prices it covered — the standard approximation. It resolves the shape of the profile, the point of control and the value area reliably; it will not match a tick-exact profile row for row. TPO counts candles touching a price, which is the same time-at-price idea a market profile is built on.

Session profiles

Pick a coin and press Build profile.
Data: Binance public REST, USD-M futures or spot. No key, no account. POC is the price row with the most volume (or most TPO). The value area is the narrowest band around the POC holding the chosen share of total activity — its edges are VAH and VAL. A naked POC is a past session's point of control that price has not traded back through since; they are listed because they are watched, not because they are proven. The in-progress candle is discarded, so nothing repaints.

Indicators — the whole panel, one coin

~45 indicators computed from live candles · classics plus the published community algorithms · nothing is fetched from TradingView
A count of agreeing indicators is not a probability. These are the standard formulas computed on closed candles — the readings are exact, the interpretation is the usual textbook one. This toolkit already measured what happens when many timeframes agree on one signal: the crowded buckets did worse than a random entry. A summary that says STRONG BUY means most formulas currently point up, and nothing more.
Pick a coin and press Read indicators.
Community algorithms are reimplemented from their published formulas — Squeeze Momentum and WaveTrend (LazyBear), SuperTrend (KivancOzbilgic), UT Bot (QuantNomad), Williams Vix Fix (ChrisMoody), and the standard smart-money building blocks (swing structure, fair value gaps). No Pine source is fetched, copied or executed; closed-source scripts cannot be reproduced at all. Values will track those indicators closely but will not always match a specific author's variant parameter for parameter.

Strategies — 56 rule sets, backtested on live candles

Every classic entry rule plus the published community ones, run over real history with fees, on the coin and timeframe you choose
This is an in-sample fit, and that is the whole problem. Every row below is one rule scored on one coin over one window it has already seen. The scanner's own signal looked like +0.18% per trade under exactly this kind of scoring, then delivered −1.06% walk-forward with 0 of 4 folds positive. Ranking 48 rules and taking the top one is the fastest way to reproduce that mistake. Read the table as a description of the past, and re-test anything promising on a window it has never touched.
Pick a coin and press Backtest all.
Execution model: a rule reads only closed candles up to bar i, and the resulting position is held through bar i+1 — no signal is ever acted on inside the bar that produced it, so nothing here is lookahead. Equity is marked to market every bar. The round-trip fee is charged as half on entry and half on exit, and a flip from long to short pays both. Community rules are reimplemented from their published formulas; no Pine source is fetched or executed.

Gold Entry Pro v4 — adaptive context + execution + trace

Adaptive scoring across multi-timeframe regime, trend/momentum, structure, strategy ensemble, OCC evidence, order flow, derivatives, BTC/ETH context, relative strength, execution liquidity and exchange events
Power v4 adds context, execution, trace and command-center memory. Regime is now read across 1h/4h/12h/1d; altcoins are compared with BTC and ETH; derivatives distinguish fresh position-building from covering/liquidation; and liquidity uses live spread, book depth and estimated $10k slippage. Gold signals are automatically paper-tracked in browser storage with entry, stop, target, MFE/MAE and lifecycle state. The score remains a research ranking, not a probability or investment recommendation.
